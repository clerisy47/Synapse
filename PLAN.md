## Next iteration — vault retrieval and answer coverage

User-directed planning revision, 2026-10-19. This iteration is partially implemented; the deterministic retrieval/packing checkpoint is recorded in [coverage evidence](docs/evidence/retrieval-coverage-2026-10-19.md). Sufficiency and final live qualification remain open. Earlier DONE entries retain their historical acceptance evidence; they do not establish adequate performance on the larger vault. Baseline: [vault evaluation](docs/evidence/test-vault-2026-10-19.md), [final results](docs/evidence/test-vault-2026-10-19.json). Only 4/10 documented questions passed requested-fact coverage; overall 15/21 checks passed. All returned citations were authentic, but six incomplete answers were labeled `answered`.

Observed causes to address:

- `Corpus::query` scores notes and selects the first matching line plus five lines. A title or generic term can select an introduction while the answer appears later.
- `ChatSession::bounded` further truncates passages to six lines within a small shared input budget. Lists, tables and comparisons can lose necessary rows or sections.
- Selection of any nonempty evidence set becomes `answered`; relevance alone is insufficient to establish coverage of the question.
- The permitted Test Queries note repeats questions and links without answering them, and sometimes occupies evidence budget. Do not hardcode its filename or silently exclude it.
- All 19 original notes lack required metadata. Successful tests on a prepared copy do not fix original-vault usability.

Keep the Rust ports/adapters architecture, no embeddings, application-owned citations, current access checks, one JobQueue and local Bonsai runtime. Improve retrieval within the 4,096-context and three-model-call ceilings before considering any budget change. No automatic metadata repair, model installation, remote fallback or original-vault edits are authorized by this plan.
